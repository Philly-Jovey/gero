Purpose of the Program

This program prepares arbitrators and mediators to resolve disputes affecting the elderly and helps build inclusive, safe communities with specialized knowledge in geriatrics and conflict management

Benefits of Becoming a G'ADR Arbitrator/Mediator

Enhanced mediation skills, Specialized gerontology knowledge, Recognition as a trained dispute resolver, More effective, compassionate service delivery.

A Legacy of Academic Partnership

Our curriculum and programs are developed in close collaboration with the Department of Social Work and Social Administration at Makerere University, ensuring every program is grounded in world-class research.
