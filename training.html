G'ADR Gerontology Training

Empowering arbitrators and mediators with specialized knowledge, skills, and confidence to serve older adults in communities across Uganda.

Bridging Wisdom & Resolution

G'ADR (Gerontological Alternative Dispute Resolution) is a pioneering program that sits at the intersection of aging science and legal mediation.

groups

What is Gerontology?

Gerontology is the study of aging, including the biological, psychological, social, and policy-related aspects of later life. It empowers professionals to better understand the needs of older adults and design supportive solution

science

Purpose of the Program

Resolving disputes affecting the elderly and building inclusive, safe communities.

August Training Schedule

Structured learning across five consecutive Saturdays at Makerere University

1st Saturday

Orientation & Foundations

Introduction to Gerontology and the landscape of elder care in Uganda.

2nd Saturday

Active Listening

Core mediation techniques and establishing rapport with older adults.

3rd Saturday

Conflict Resolution

Elder Rights & Protection strategies in complex family disputes.

4th Saturday

Ethics & Practice

Case Management protocols and ethical considerations in geriatrics.

5th Saturday

Certification

Final Assessment, Certification & Practicum at Makerere University.
